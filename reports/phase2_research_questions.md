# Phase 2 (mock): Research Questions

| # | Question | Type | Measure | Issue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RQ1 | How large is the 2025-26 shortfall in visitors and RevPAR compared with what the pre-2025 trend and seasonality predict? | Quantitative | gap between actual and forecast (seasonal model trained on 2015-2019 and 2022-2024) | 1 |
| RQ2 | Did Downtown decline more than the Strip and other Nevada areas over the same months? | Quantitative | difference-in-differences on occupancy and gaming win by area | 2 |
| RQ3 | Which guest and hotel characteristics are associated with lower guest ratings? | Quantitative (+ qualitative if review text is available) | regression on review scores; theme counts in review text | 3 |
| RQ4 | How much of the 2025 drop lines up with the fall in Canadian and international visitors and in air capacity? | Quantitative | decomposition of the visitor change by origin; correlation with airport passengers | 4 |

## Justifications

- **RQ1** separates a real shortfall from normal seasonality and calendar effects; without it, a single bad month can be mistaken for a trend.
- **RQ2** uses other areas as a comparison group, which is the closest the public data gets to asking whether Downtown has its own problem.
- **RQ3** tests the price/value story with guest-level data rather than headlines.
- **RQ4** sizes the external causes, so recommendations do not target something the destination cannot control.
